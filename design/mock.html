How levelling works

  1. Your quest list is yours. Water, reading, walking, cooking, anything. Each has its own reminder times, saved once.
  2. Each morning you pick. If you ignore the morning call, yesterday's picks carry over so the day is never empty.
  3. Every tick pays XP. Partial progress pays too (each glass of water counts). Clearing every quest adds a bonus.
  4. Consistency multiplies XP. Each streak day adds 2%, up to +50%. A missed day never takes XP away; it only resets the multiplier unless a rest token covers it.
  5. Levels get slower, ranks mark the big steps. E, D, C, B, A, S.
  6. Your partner is a side layer. You see each other's level and progress, can poke, and share a couple streak. Nothing of yours depends on it.
